What to take from the data

Observations drawn directly from the sheet. They describe this set of 18 sites at one point in time and are not forecasts.

Authority is the clearest signal

AS tracks traffic at +0.95, and the top four sites (AS 42 and above) hold 88.5% of it. Nobody outside that group draws more than 33.4K.

Link volume is a weak guide

Backlinks track traffic at only +0.47. Blacklane gets 209 visits per backlink, airportstaxitransfers.com gets 0.08. Link count alone does not explain who ranks.

Content depth goes with the leaders

Four of the five biggest traffic sites run 780 or more pages, while 8 of 18 sites have fewer than 100.

The big keywords are not locked

Only three sites hold #1 above 5K searches. “Near me” terms are the soft spot: four of seven are held below #1, two of them at 9.9K searches.

Reputation and visibility are separate

Ratings run from 1.9 to 4.8 with a median of 4.5, yet rating shows no positive link to traffic (-0.35). Two big names sit at 1.9.

Google Business Profile effort is thin

Only Minicabs-UK posts every second day, and 4 of the 9 sites with cadence data post once a month or less. Nine sites have no cadence recorded.

Airport work is the baseline

17 of 18 sites list it. Heathrow is named by 8 sites, Gatwick and Stansted by 7 each, and London City by only 3.

Niche services are lightly covered

School runs and hospital transfers (3 sites each), wheelchair access (3) and child seats (2) are listed by very few competitors.

Source and method

Every figure in this deck comes from one sheet: Competitors Analysis Sheet – AC (Google Sheets). The values are used as recorded and were not independently re-checked.

Calculated here: share of combined traffic, visits per backlink (traffic divided by backlinks), visits per page (traffic divided by total pages), medians and means, and Spearman rank correlations (with the sample size shown on the slide).

Groups: AS 40 and above, AS 20 to 39, and AS under 20. The group names (scale players, rising mid-tier, local long tail) are descriptive labels added for this deck.

Service types were grouped by keyword from the Services column, so one listed service can fall in more than one type.

Approximations: posting-cadence bar lengths convert the sheet’s wording (such as “Every 2nd day”) into rough posts a month. Where two founded years are given, both are shown and the first is used for ordering. minicabit’s “4 star” is read as 4.0.

Abbreviations: AS Authority Score, SV search volume, POS ranking position, GBP Google Business Profile.
